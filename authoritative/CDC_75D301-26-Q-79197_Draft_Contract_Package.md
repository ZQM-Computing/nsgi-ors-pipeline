SECTION 2 — TECHNICAL PROPOSAL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2.1 UNDERSTANDING OF THE REQUIREMENT

The CDC Office of Acquisition Services seeks subscription-based access to a mature, commercially available visual analytics software capability designed to support a range of visual analytic workflows. This is a brand-name or equivalent acquisition for i2 Analyst's Notebook. The Government requires i2 Analyst's Notebook to meet its minimum requirements for a dedicated link analysis and visualization capability installed on Government devices, including native link/relationship charting, structured analytic objects, timelines, and exportable analytic products.

[YOUR FIRM NAME] understands the requirement as follows:

a) Software: i2 Analyst's Notebook concurrent user licenses, installed on CDC devices
b) Subscription: Annual software subscription including all updates and upgrades
c) Support: 12 months of technical support and maintenance
d) Training: Onboarding training for CDC analysts on i2 Analyst's Notebook usage
e) Enablement: Configuration assistance, best-practice guidance, and template development
f) AI Compliance: Full compliance with CDC/HHS AI Use Compliance and Risk Management Plan requirements

2.2 TECHNICAL APPROACH

PHASE 1 — LICENSE PROCUREMENT AND INSTALLATION
- Procure i2 Analyst's Notebook concurrent user licenses under GSA Schedule contract authority
- Coordinate with CDC IT for installation on Government devices
- Verify license activation and system compatibility
- Provide installation documentation and acceptance testing

PHASE 2 — ENABLEMENT AND CONFIGURATION
- Configure i2 Analyst's Notebook to CDC analytic workflows
- Develop custom templates for link analysis, timeline analysis, and relationship charting
- Establish data import/export protocols aligned with CDC data governance requirements
- Integrate with existing CDC data sources as authorized

PHASE 3 — TRAINING AND KNOWLEDGE TRANSFER
- Conduct initial training sessions for CDC analysts (onsite or virtual as authorized)
- Develop training materials: user guides, quick reference cards, video tutorials
- Provide train-the-trainer sessions for CDC super-users
- Document training completion and competency assessments

PHASE 4 — ONGOING SUPPORT
- Provide 12 months of software subscription and technical support
- Respond to support requests within agreed SLA (4 business hours critical, 1 business day standard)
- Apply software updates and patches as released by i2 Group
- Quarterly health checks and optimization reviews

2.3 AI USE COMPLIANCE INTEGRATION

All deployment activities integrate the requirements of the CDC Artificial Intelligence (AI) Use Compliance and Risk Management Plan:
- AI tool inventory registration

- Annual compliance review cycle
